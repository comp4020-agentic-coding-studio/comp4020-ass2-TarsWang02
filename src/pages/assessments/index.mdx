Three pieces of work, weighted 20/30/50, each building on the shared jump
engine from the [Jump Lab](/jump-lab/): a measurement-based comparison of two
supplied jumps, a configured and defended jump of your own, and a final
three-preset study with a real play-test. All three have a complete
non-coding route through the Lab's visual controls.
