## Preparation

Complete the week 10 lecture task before this session.

## Activity

1. Record a configuration A. Run a clean single-variable B comparison,
   changing exactly one parameter, and report both sides' height,
   displacement and airtime.
2. Now deliberately break the rule: from the same A, change two parameters
   at once for a second B and replay it. Try to say which of the two changes
   caused the difference you see — notice that you can't, honestly.
3. Discuss: your final assessment asks for one single-variable comparison.
   Why would a marker reject the two-variable version from step 2 even if
   its result looked more dramatic?

## Output

Both comparisons' results and the one-sentence usability judgement from the
spec above.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that the single-variable comparison in
step 1 changed exactly one parameter from A — this is the exact standard
One Body, Three Personalities applies to its required A/B comparison.
