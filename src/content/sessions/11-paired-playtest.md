## Preparation

Complete the week 11 lecture task before this session.

## Activity

1. In pairs, take turns being the player and the observer. The player gets
   no explanation beyond "here are the controls." The observer stays silent
   and takes notes for the full attempt.
2. Compare the observer's three observations against the player's own
   account of what they were trying to do — note any observation the player
   disagrees with, or anything the player noticed that the observer missed.
3. Swap roles and repeat with a different preset.

## Output

Both rounds' observation sets, interpretation sentences, and the player's
one-thing-they-didn't-understand note from the spec above.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that observations and interpretations
stayed separated — a mixed note ("they enjoyed the floaty jump") is exactly
the failure mode this week is rehearsing against, ahead of the mandatory
play-test in the final assessment.
