## Preparation

Complete the week 4 lecture task before this session — bring your two-gravity
table with you.

## Activity

1. In pairs, agree on a launch speed to hold fixed. Each person independently
   picks a different pair of gravity values and runs three trials at each.
2. Compare tables. If your airtime numbers moved in opposite directions from
   what the other person expected, figure out why before assuming a mistake
   — check the trial log's snapshotted values first, since a slider dragged
   mid-air would explain a mismatch without either of you having made an
   error.
3. As a pair, write one sentence predicting what would happen to airtime if
   you doubled gravity and doubled launch speed at the same time, then test
   it. This previews the kind of single-variable-at-a-time discipline week
   10 covers formally.

## Output

Your two-gravity trial table (four-plus trials), the averages, and the
one-sentence prediction-versus-result note from step 3.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that your reported values match the trial
log's snapshotted numbers, not the slider positions at the moment you wrote
them down — this is the same reproducibility check that carries into Take
Control, due week 6.
