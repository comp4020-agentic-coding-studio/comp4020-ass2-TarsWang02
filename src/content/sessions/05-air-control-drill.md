## Preparation

Complete the week 5 lecture task before this session.

## Activity

1. Pick three air control values spanning the slider's range. Holding the
   same direction for the whole jump each time, run three trials per value
   and log height, displacement and airtime.
2. Confirm for yourself that height and airtime stay roughly flat across the
   three values while displacement moves — if any of your height values
   jumped noticeably, check whether a different slider got bumped between
   trials before concluding air control caused it.
3. Discuss in pairs: if you were designing a precise-platforming level around
   a low air control preset, what would that force a level designer to get
   right before the player even leaves the ground? Write down one concrete
   consequence.

## Output

Your three-value displacement/height/airtime table and the one-sentence
design consequence from step 3.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will spot-check that height and airtime are actually
flat across your three air control values — this is the "variable-height
and air control" evidence Take Control, due next week, asks you to have
already gathered.
