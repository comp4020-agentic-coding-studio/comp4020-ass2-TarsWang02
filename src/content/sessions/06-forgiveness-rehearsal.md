## Preparation

Complete the week 6 lecture task before this session, including at least one
coyote-time and one jump-buffer attempt.

## Activity

1. In the Gravity Lab, use "Copy configuration" to capture the exact settings
   you're about to test, and paste that link into your notes before running
   anything — this is the reproducibility habit Take Control marks directly.
2. Run your release-cut comparison and both forgiveness trials again under
   the captured configuration, this time timing your mistiming attempts with
   a visible clock or a counted beat so the millisecond figure you report
   isn't a guess.
3. Swap your configuration link with a partner. Each of you loads the
   other's link and attempts to reproduce their forgiveness outcome. Report
   whether it matched.

## Output

Your configuration link, the release-cut height comparison, both forgiveness
trials with millisecond estimates and outcomes, and one sentence on whether
your partner reproduced your result.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that the configuration link actually
reproduces the settings you reported using it for — this is exactly what
Take Control, due Friday, is marked on.
