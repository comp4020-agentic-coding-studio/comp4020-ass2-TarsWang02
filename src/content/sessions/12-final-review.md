## Preparation

Bring whatever you have so far toward One Body, Three Personalities,
including the week 12 lecture task's two-paragraph outline.

## Activity

1. In small groups, each person presents their three-preset table and A/B
   comparison. The group checks: are all three presets backed by real
   logged trials, not remembered numbers, and does the A/B comparison change
   exactly one variable from a saved configuration link.
2. Read out your playtest observation from week 11 (or a new one if you
   haven't run it yet) and the revision it prompted. The group checks that
   the revision responds to something the observer actually wrote down, not
   a general impression.
3. Identify, as a group, the one section of the final report each person is
   least confident about, and get one piece of concrete feedback on it
   before the session ends.

## Output

Your reviewed three-preset table, A/B comparison, playtest note and
revision, plus the one piece of feedback you received in step 3.

## Criteria

Not marked — this is a rehearsal session ahead of the final submission. Your
tutor circulates to spot-check that reported numbers trace back to actual
logged trials rather than recollection, since that traceability is exactly
what the final assessment is marked on.
