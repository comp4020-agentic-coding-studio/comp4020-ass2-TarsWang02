## Preparation

Complete the week 9 lecture task before this session.

## Activity

1. Without telling your partner whether sound will be on, have them run a
   trial muted and write their takeoff/landing/outcome description before
   any sound plays.
2. Replay the same input pattern with sound on and compare against their
   written description. Note any mismatch.
3. Swap roles, then as a pair try to find the mute control without being
   told where it is. Time how long it took and note anything confusing
   about it.

## Output

The muted-trial description, its sound-on comparison, and the mute-control
findability note from step 3.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that the muted description alone was
sufficient to reconstruct the trial's outcome — a description that had to
guess is evidence of an audio-only information leak, which this course's
sound rules are designed to prevent.
