## Preparation

Read the week 3 lecture and open the [Jump Lab](/jump-lab/) once beforehand.
Pick two presets you're curious about — they don't have to be the ones the
assessment uses.

## Activity

1. Choose two presets. Run three immediate-mode trials of each, logging
   height, displacement and airtime from the Jump Lab's trial log rather
   than transcribing by hand.
2. Write the two-line method statement from the lecture task, then swap it
   — not your table — with a partner. Each of you runs the *other's* method
   statement against your own copy of the Jump Lab and compares your result
   to their table.
3. As a group: report one method statement that turned out to be missing
   something (a mode, a trial count, which preset) once someone else tried
   to follow it literally. This is the failure mode Jump Autopsy is graded
   against, and it's much cheaper to hit here than in a submission.

## Output

Your two-jump table, method statement and the one-sentence comparison from
the spec above, handed to your tutor at the end of the session.

## Criteria

Not marked, but checked for the same thing Jump Autopsy checks: can your
method statement be followed by someone who wasn't in the room. If your
partner's attempt to reproduce your table landed outside your own three-trial
spread, find out why before the assessment is due Friday.
