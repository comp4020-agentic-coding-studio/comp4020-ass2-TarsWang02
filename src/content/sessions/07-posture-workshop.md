## Preparation

Complete the week 7 lecture task and watch the linked slides before this
session.

## Activity

1. For each of the three physics presets (Floaty, Heavy, Precise), choose a
   pose setting you think matches its physics feel, using the posture
   comparison's fixed trajectory so you're only judging the body, not the
   path.
2. Show your three choices to a partner without telling them which pose was
   meant for which preset. Ask them to guess. Record whether they guessed
   correctly, and if not, what pose feature misled them.
3. Revise the pose that was guessed wrong, if any, and note what you
   changed.

## Output

Your three pose settings, their justification sentences, your partner's
guesses and outcomes, and any revision from step 3. This directly rehearses
the pose choices One Body, Three Personalities asks you to defend in week
12.

## Criteria

Not marked. Your tutor will check that all three comparisons used the same
recorded trajectory — a pose that "worked" because the trajectory quietly
changed underneath it doesn't count as a pose result.
